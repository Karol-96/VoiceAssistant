  - Nepal's Panchang Nirnayak (Bikas) Samiti fixes the dates and auspicious times (sait). - **2083 (2026/27) holiday pattern.**

  - Tihar: five days, from Laxmi Puja to the day after Bhai Tika.
  - Source: [Nepal HRM 2083/84 holiday calendar](https://nepalhrm.com/tools/holiday-calendar/), as attributed in the search summary.

### Inferences
- **Own tithi calculation, Dashain 2027:**
  - New moon is Thu 30 Sep 2027, 08:20 NPT. Ashwin Shukla Pratipada runs from 08:21 Thu 30 Sep to 05:51 Fri 1 Oct.
  - Pratipada never touches a sunrise (a *kshaya* tithi), so **30 Sep is the only possible Ghatasthapana day**. The Ghatasthapana sait should fall after about 08:21 that morning.
  - Saptami is the sunrise tithi on Wed 6 Oct (**Fulpati ✓**).
  - Ashtami runs 04:29 Thu 7 Oct to 06:43 Fri 8 Oct. Navami runs 06:43 Fri 8 Oct to 09:18 Sat 9 Oct. **Dashami runs 09:18 Sat 9 Oct to 11:56 Sun 10 Oct.**
- **Why the Tika date splits (9 vs 10 Oct):**
  - Dashami is present at sunrise only on **Sun 10 Oct**. It covers the afternoon (aparahna) only on Sat 9 Oct.
  - India's Dussehra uses the afternoon rule, which gives 9 Oct and explains Drik Panchang's date.
- **Backtest on 2026:**
  - The afternoon rule gives 20 Oct 2026; the sunrise rule gives 21 Oct 2026. Nepal's official Tika was 21 Oct 2026, so Nepal follows the sunrise rule.
  - The same method also reproduces Ghatasthapana (11 Oct 2026) and Bhai Tika (11 Nov 2026).
  - **Conclusion: Dashain Tika 2027 = Sunday 10 October 2027.** Confidence is high, pending the official notice.
- **Own calculation, Tihar 2027:**

  | Tihar day | Date | Sunrise tithi |
  |---|---|---|
  | Kag Tihar | Wed 27 Oct | Krishna Trayodashi |
  | Kukur Tihar | Thu 28 Oct | Chaturdashi |
  | Gai Tihar / Laxmi Puja | Fri 29 Oct | Amavasya |
  | Govardhan / Mha Puja | Sat 30 Oct | Pratipada |
  | Bhai Tika | Sun 31 Oct | Dwitiya |

  - Amavasya runs 21:04 Thu 28 Oct to 19:22 Fri 29 Oct, so the sunrise rule and the evening (pradosh) rule both give 29 Oct. This date is robust.
- **Own calculation, Chhath:** Shashthi runs 19:47 Wed 3 Nov to 21:54 Thu 4 Nov. That puts the evening offering on **Thu 4 Nov** and the dawn offering on **Fri 5 Nov 2027**.
- **Kojagrat Purnima:** Purnima is the sunrise tithi on Fri 15 Oct (full moon 19:31 NPT), so Kojagrat falls about 14–15 Oct.
- **Likely 2027 public-holiday blocks** (by analogy with the 2083 pattern):
  - Dashain: about **Wed 6 Oct to Tue 12 Oct 2027**. Dwadashi is the sunrise tithi on Tue 12 Oct.
  - Tihar: about **Fri 29 Oct to Mon 1 Nov 2027**.
  - Chhath: Thu 4 Nov.
- **Air-travel demand windows this implies:**
  - Outbound peak to KTM: about 18 Sep to 6 Oct 2027.
  - Post-Tika return wave: about 11–17 Oct.
  - Post-Tihar/Chhath return wave: about 1–8 Nov 2027.

### Gaps
- No official BS 2084 panchanga, government holiday notice or Tika sait was found. The 2026 sait was only reported on 7 Oct 2026 ([ShareSansar](https://www.sharesansar.com/newsdetail/dashain-tika-sait-fixed-for-1026-am-on-kartik-4-2026-10-07)), so 2027's will likely come in about September 2027.
- Only one Bikram Sambat conversion was sourced (Ashoj 24, 2084 = 10 Oct 2027). Other BS dates are not verified.
- Drik Panchang pages could not be opened directly; their dates come from search summaries.

## 2. When are the late-September/October 2027 spring school holidays (and the December 2027 summer holidays) in each Australian state?

### Takeaway
The 2027 spring break splits the states into two groups:
- **VIC, QLD and (reportedly) NT** break early, about Sat 18 Sep to Sun 3/Mon 4 Oct. That covers only Ghatasthapana.
- **NSW, SA, WA, TAS and ACT** break Sat 25 Sep to Sun 10 Oct (ACT to Mon 11 Oct). That covers Ghatasthapana through Navami, but **Tika (Sun 10 Oct) is the last day of the break.**

Tihar (27–31 Oct) and Chhath (4 Nov) fall in Term 4 in every state. Summer holidays begin Sat 11 Dec (QLD, SA), Sat 18 Dec (VIC) and Tue 21 Dec (NSW).

### Inferences
- **How each state's break lines up with Dashain** (Ghatasthapana 30 Sep, Fulpati 6 Oct, Ashtami/Navami 7–8 Oct, Tika 10 Oct, Kojagrat about 15 Oct):
  - **NSW, SA, WA, TAS:** the break covers Ghatasthapana to Navami.
    - Tika falls on the break's final Sunday. A family attending Tika could fly out on the night of 10 Oct, but would land on 11/12 Oct and probably miss 1–2 school days.
    - Staying on to Kojagrat means missing a week.
  - **ACT:** the same, with one extra day (Term 4 starts Tue 12 Oct).
  - **VIC, QLD, NT:** the break ends 3–4 Oct, so it covers Ghatasthapana only. Attending Fulpati to Tika means about a week out of school (VIC 4–8 Oct; QLD 5–8 Oct).
- **Tihar and Chhath** fall in Term 4 in every state. School-age families would need 1–2 weeks of leave. The Tihar-only market is more likely adults, students, retirees or families willing to take children out of school.
- **Two outbound peaks:** about Sat 18 Sep (VIC, QLD, NT) and about Sat 25 Sep (NSW, SA, WA, TAS, ACT). Both sit in the busiest pre-Ghatasthapana week for diaspora travel to Kathmandu.
- **December 2027:**
  - Outbound waves start Sat 11 Dec (QLD, SA), Sat 18 Dec (VIC) and Tue 21 Dec (NSW).
  - Return waves run late January 2028: QLD back Mon 24 Jan; VIC 27–28 Jan; NSW after 28 Jan (Eastern division) or 4 Feb (Western division).
- **Suggested benchmark itineraries for the group-fare comparison:**
  - Dashain + Tihar: out Thu 23 / Sat 25 Sep 2027, back Mon 1 – Sun 7 Nov 2027.
  - Dashain only: out Sat 25 Sep, back Sun 10 / Sat 16 Oct.
  - Tihar: out Fri 22 / Sat 23 Oct, back Mon 1 – Sun 7 Nov.
  - December: out Fri 17 / Sat 18 Dec 2027, back Sat 15 – Sun 23 Jan 2028.

### Gaps
- Term 4 end dates and summer holidays were not found for WA, TAS, ACT and NT.
- NT spring dates come from a single commercial source.
- The official NSW Term 3/4 lines were unreadable in the extract. The QLD spring dates come from a state school's page, not the department page itself.
- Non-government schools set their own terms and may differ.

## 4. Which airlines and routings serve Australia to Kathmandu today (connections, durations, hubs; direct or one-stop by city; KTM, BWA, PKR)?

### Takeaway
There is no nonstop flight and no Nepali-carrier service from Australia; every trip connects at least once. The one-stop gateways are:
- Singapore: Singapore Airlines, daily SIN–KTM.
- Kuala Lumpur: Malaysia Airlines and Batik Air.
- Bangkok: Thai.
- Hong Kong: Cathay, daily wide-body, resumed 1 Oct 2025.
- Guangzhou: China Southern.
- Delhi: Air India, from SYD and MEL.
- Gulf hubs: Qatar and Emirates, with a long backtrack.

One-stop journeys are about 15–19 hours. Two-stop itineraries run up to 26–36+ hours.

Canberra and the Gold Coast have no confirmed one-stop Asian-hub link. Qatar's Canberra–Doha service starts 8 Dec 2026, and AirAsia X's Gold Coast–KL status for 2026 is unconfirmed. Bhairahawa has had no scheduled international flights since March 2026, and Pokhara has only flydubai (daily from 23 Sep 2026). **Kathmandu is effectively the only gateway.**

- **Cheapest options:** a mix of two-stop, self-transfer or low-cost builds and Singapore Airlines sale fares.

  - **SQ one-stop promotions of AUD 845–891 return have undercut** the low-cost builds (Batik from about $1,019 return).
  - Off-peak, the cheapest fares run about AUD 850–1,200 return.
- **Reasonable one-stop options:** full-service tickets at non-sale prices (SQ via Singapore, MH via KL, CX via Hong Kong, TG via Bangkok) start about AUD 1,000–1,450 return off-peak and rise with the season.
- **Gulf one-stops:** Qatar and Emirates are one-stop but involve a long backtrack. Emirates is pricier (about AUD 1,660 on Expedia US for SYD).

### Inferences
- **What "book it yourself" should mean in the comparison.** Compare a group fare against a **one-stop full-service return** (SQ, MH, CX or TG), not against the cheapest two-stop or self-transfer build:
  - Groups typically include families, elders and first-time travellers, and two-stop builds add 5–10+ hours.
  - Self-transfers and two separate one-ways carry misconnection risk in the festival peak.
- **Illustrative estimates for festival dates** (ESTIMATE; Section 3 method):
  - Cheapest end: about AUD 1,250–1,450 in October and about AUD 1,330–1,550 in December from SYD, MEL or PER.
  - Reasonable one-stop: about AUD 1,430–1,840 in October and about AUD 1,530–2,190 in December, depending on city.
- Because one-ways cost about 45–55% of a return, travellers can mix carriers, for example out on Batik and back on SQ. The individual benchmark is then about twice a peak-date one-way, which may exceed a return booked early.

### Gaps
- No same-date pairs of one-way and return fares exist for the 2027 festival dates. The ratios above come from mixed, undated off-peak snapshots.
- Baggage allowances were not captured. They vary by carrier and fare brand, and matter for diaspora travellers carrying gifts. Change and refund conditions were not captured either.
