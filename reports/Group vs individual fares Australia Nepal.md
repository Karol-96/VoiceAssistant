# Group fares buy time, not cheaper seats

No: on the evidence available, an airline group fare to Kathmandu does not reliably beat the best fare an individual can book alone from any of the eight Australian cities, and for Dashain, Tihar and December 2027 it is more likely to match an early individual fare than to undercut it. The first claim holds. The cheapest fares in this market, **AUD 845–964 return** in Singapore Airlines and Malaysia Airlines sales run in 2025–26, go to individuals for mostly off-peak dates, while airlines price a group from the revenue its seats would otherwise earn, which puts a quote nearer the route average (about AUD 1,360 from Melbourne) than the sale floor, and higher still on peak dates. The second claim is half right. Individual fares do jump: October averages run **26–43%** above the July–August low, December **20–70%**, and a late booking costs about **10–20%** more than an early one in a normal year and far more in a stressed one, as November 2024's **+34% to +155%** Kathmandu–Sydney spike showed. But Dashain and Tihar 2027 flights are already on sale to individuals, and booking early does not escape the festival premium, which airlines build into their fares from the start; what a group adds is a cheaper, looser lock, with about **10% of the fare at risk** per seat and names due three to six weeks before departure, instead of roughly AUD 1,300–1,950 paid upfront in each traveller's name. For SaatSamundra that means a real but modest price edge, about **AUD 150–300 a seat** against families who would otherwise book in the last two months and little or nothing against families who book early on their own, available only when ten or more people share one flight and the quote comes in at or below the same-day individual fare; family drop-outs, fixed dates, agent-only access to the main Asian carriers and disruption in Nepal are the risks. **This research obtained no airline group quote and no live Google Flights fare for the 2027 dates**; the final section gives a quote request ready to send to three airlines and the exact searches and benchmarks needed to finish the test.

## Claim one holds: AUD 845–964 sale fares go to individuals, not groups

The lowest dated return fares of the past year were airline sales sold seat by seat. Malaysia Airlines' July 2026 sale priced Kathmandu at **AUD 853 return from Perth, 887 from Brisbane and 964 from Adelaide**, checked bag and meals included, with the cheapest dates in February and March 2027 ([iwantthatflight](https://iwantthatflight.com.au/deal/nepal-on-your-bucket-list-flights-with-malaysia-airlines-from-853-return)), and a second round followed from AUD 879 in August ([iwantthatflight](https://iwantthatflight.com.au/deal/peak-plans-flights-to-nepal-with-malaysia-airlines-from-879-return)). Singapore Airlines' Nepal sales ran **AUD 845–891 return** for 2025–26 travel ([iwantthatflight](https://iwantthatflight.com.au/deal/altitude-meets-attitude-singapore-airlines-flights-to-nepal-from-845-return); [I Know The Pilot](https://iknowthepilot.com.au/nepal-on-your-mind-singapore-airlines-flights-from-891-return-let-s-climb)), and aggregator teasers dip lower still, to AUD 713 from Perth, without dates or conditions ([Skyscanner AU](https://www.skyscanner.com.au/routes/per/ktm/perth-to-kathmandu.html)). The sales sit **9–16% below** the standard fares Singapore Airlines lists for the same cities, from AUD 1,001 out of Sydney, 1,051 from Brisbane, 935 from Perth and 1,089 from Adelaide ([Singapore Airlines Sydney](https://www.singaporeair.com/en-au/flights-from-sydney-to-kathmandu); [Brisbane](https://www.singaporeair.com/en-au/flights-from-brisbane-to-kathmandu); [Perth](https://www.singaporeair.com/en-au/flights-from-perth-to-kathmandu); [Adelaide](https://www.singaporeair.com/en-au/flights-to-kathmandu)), and **29–38% below** Melbourne's average fare, which Kayak's figures put near AUD 1,360: August, the cheapest month, averages AUD 1,118 and is "18% less expensive on average" ([Kayak AU](https://www.kayak.com.au/flight-routes/Melbourne-MEL/Kathmandu-Tribhuvan-KTM)).

Group pricing starts somewhere else. The classic model sets the minimum group fare at the revenue the airline loses by displacing individual passengers ([Svrcek, MIT](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/68097)), and under bid-price control a booking is rejected whenever its fare falls below the summed bid prices of the flight legs it uses ([McGill and van Ryzin](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/3925/vanryzin_revenue.pdf)). The leading commercial tool, PROS Group Sales Optimizer, prices each request from the marginal cost of the seats and markets itself to airlines on a **15% lift in group revenue** ([PROS](https://pros.com/products/airline-group-sales-software/)): software built to make groups pay more, not less. Australian agencies put it plainly, warning that a group quote "may reflect the carrier's expected average fare for the route rather than the lowest fare currently visible online" and that promotional fares "are usually sold to individual bookings" ([SkyTrips](https://skytrips.com.au/blog/group-vs-individual-flight-booking-price-comparison); [Travel Agent Finder](https://www.travelagentfinder.com.au/blog/group-airfares-are-they-really-cheaper)). Airlines' own small-party deals follow the same rule; Thai's two-person "Fly with Love" fare is sold only on its website and app ([Thai Airways](https://www.thaiairways.com/en-au/content/offers-Promotions/special-offers/fly-with-love/)). Even a family booking together gets pushed up, because when the cheapest fare bucket cannot seat the whole party, everyone moves to the next one ([Dollar Flight Club](https://dollarflightclub.com/articles/book-flights-large-groups-without-overpaying/)); in one illustration, three seats bought together cost $250 each against $200 bought singly, a **25% penalty** ([Explore](https://www.explore.com/1389600/can-buying-airplane-tickets-individually-group-savings/)).

Put together, the claim holds with room to spare. Taking Melbourne's average as the yardstick, a group quote set at the expected average fare would sit **about AUD 400–500 a seat, or 40–60%, above the 2026 sale fares**. On peak dates the gap widens; the only quantified estimate found puts group fares **about 9% above individual fares on peak dates**, though it comes from a vendor blog citing a report that could not be traced ([futureflights.ai](https://futureflights.ai/efficient-group-flight-booking)). The strongest counter-case is off-peak travel, when displacement costs are low and a group can be priced cheaply, but that is exactly when sale fares are most available, so the group's advantage is squeezed from both ends. Sales rarely reach the homecoming peak: Cathay and Qatar both ran Dashain 2026 promotions out of Nepal, and Qatar's blacked out inbound travel on 1–8 November, the Tihar return ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/10/20/the-dashain-break-is-a-chance-to-see-more-of-nepal-and-beyond); [Qatar Airways](https://www.qatarairways.com/en-np/offers/dashain-offer.html)). When a sale does reach a festival window, as Singapore Airlines' 2025 "Dream Big" fares on its Nepal site did with Australia travel windows of 12 September–4 October 2025 and 21 November 2025–11 January 2026 ([Singapore Airlines Nepal](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/np/plan-travel/local-promotions/dream-big-this-2025)), a displacement-priced group quote cannot compete. The proof is structural, not side by side: no group quote for this route was available to set against a sale fare, and the final section sets up exactly that test.

## Claim two is half right: fares jump, but anyone can lock in today

Individual fares do jump for the festivals and the Australian summer. On monthly averages, October, the Dashain and Tihar month in most years, runs **26–43% above the July–August low**: Kayak's Melbourne figures imply October about 26% above August ([Kayak AU](https://www.kayak.com.au/flight-routes/Melbourne-MEL/Kathmandu-Tribhuvan-KTM)), its Kathmandu–Sydney data shows an average 32% rise in October ([Kayak UK](https://kayak.co.uk/flight-routes/Kathmandu-Tribhuvan-KTM/Sydney-Kingsford-Smith-SYD)), and its Sydney averages give October £779 against August £546, a 43% gap ([Kayak UK](https://www.kayak.co.uk/flight-routes/Sydney-Kingsford-Smith-SYD/Kathmandu-Tribhuvan-KTM)). December is steeper, at **20–70% above off-peak**. Thai's Sydney fares start at AUD 1,680 for December 2026 against AUD 1,405 for late April to mid-May 2027 ([Thai Airways](https://www.thaiairways.com/flights/en/flights-from-sydney-to-kathmandu); [Thai Airways](https://www.thaiairways.com/flights/en/flights-from-australia-to-nepal)), Google Flights' Sydney route page shows December typically at A$1,600–2,600 against A$1,150–1,400 in February ([Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-sydney-to-kathmandu.html?gl=AU&hl=en)), and Kayak's December average is 70% above August ([Kayak UK](https://www.kayak.co.uk/flight-routes/Sydney-Kingsford-Smith-SYD/Kathmandu-Tribhuvan-KTM)). The January return leg peaks too, at 34% above June on Kayak's Kathmandu–Sydney averages, though one of three Kayak readings disagrees ([Kayak](https://www.kayak.com/flight-routes/Kathmandu-Tribhuvan-KTM/Sydney-Kingsford-Smith-SYD)). A stressed season goes much further. In November 2024, with Kathmandu's airport about to close overnight and Sydney the busiest route out of Kathmandu after Delhi, a Kathmandu–Sydney fare of Rs85,000 on normal days reached **Rs114,000–217,000, or +34% to +155%**, about AUD 1,295–2,466 ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2024/10/29/kathmandu-airport-s-reduced-hours-send-airfares-into-orbit)). Nepal's travel trade said Dashain 2026 international fares out of Kathmandu were **at least 30% above 2025** ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/10/20/the-dashain-break-is-a-chance-to-see-more-of-nepal-and-beyond)), and Nepali-Australian guides put festival-month returns at AUD 1,400–1,900, on flights that "book out months in advance" ([Stunning Nepal](https://www.stunningnepal.com/australia-to-nepal/); [SkyTrips](https://skytrips.com.au/blog/nepal-airlines-direct-flight-sydney-2026-status)).

Two parts of the claim do not survive. First, locking in early is not a group privilege. Published booking windows run 353–361 days, measured on award seats ([Point Hacks](https://pointhacks.com.au/when-do-award-calendars-open/); [Executive Traveller](https://executivetraveller.com/when-do-award-calendars-open)), so a Dashain 2027 trip leaving on 18–25 September, 302–309 days from today, is already within reach of any individual, as are Tihar returns up to about 8–16 November 2027. December 2027 departures open in the second half of December 2026, and a full December–January return can first be priced around 19–27 January 2027. No airline publishes how far ahead it will quote a group, although Singapore Airlines' terms provide for bookings made more than 90 days out ([Singapore Airlines](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/gb/plan-travel/local-promotions/groups/IATA-Agents-TnC)). Second, an early lock does not escape the festival premium, because airlines build it into advance fares. Cathay listed Brisbane–Kathmandu from **AUD 1,462 for late October to early December 2026**, 11–27% above its AUD 1,149–1,319 "from" fares on the route ([Cathay Pacific](https://flights.cathaypacific.com/destinations/en_AU/flights-from-brisbane-to-kathmandu)), and Singapore Airlines listed Canberra from **AUD 1,625 for the Dashain 2025 window**, 18.5% above Canberra's AUD 1,371 low for February–March 2027 ([Singapore Airlines](https://singaporeair.com/en-sg/flights-from-canberra-to-nepal); [iwantthatflight](https://iwantthatflight.com.au/cheap-flights/CBR/KTM/canberra-to-kathmandu)). What an early booking avoids is the late climb, which route data puts at **about 10–20%** in ordinary conditions: Kayak finds Brisbane–Kathmandu about 20% cheaper booked 14 weeks out than at the last minute ([Kayak UK](https://www.kayak.co.uk/flight-routes/Brisbane-BNE/Kathmandu-Tribhuvan-KTM)), Expedia puts the saving at 10% for booking two months ahead ([Expedia](https://www.expedia.ca/fr/lp/vols/mel/ktm/melbourne-a-kathmandou)), and Kayak's cheapest point is 23 weeks out ([Kayak AU](https://kayak.com.au/flight-routes/Brisbane-BNE/Kathmandu-Tribhuvan-KTM)). No source shows booking 10–12 months ahead beating three to six months ahead. For festival dates the case for going early is insurance against sell-outs: fares into Kathmandu start climbing around July and rise sharply in September and October ([Flights Nepal](https://flightsnepal.com/blog/dashain-tihar-flight-booking/)), and on Nepal's own routes cheap fares vanish once Ghatasthapana begins ([Nepal News](https://english.nepalnews.com/s/business/cheap-air-tickets-disappear-leaving-dashain-travelers-with-costly-options/)).

### A group lock puts about 10% of the fare at risk instead of 100%

What a group adds is the terms of the lock, not the lock itself. An individual who wants a Dashain seat today buys the ticket outright, at the full fare, in each traveller's name. Singapore Airlines' published group terms, written for its global network and not confirmed for Australian sales, instead calculate a **10% deposit that is collected only if seats are cancelled** after the deposit deadline, allow free cancellation until then, and need names only **42 days before departure** ([Singapore Airlines](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/gb/plan-travel/local-promotions/groups/IATA-Agents-TnC)). Qantas takes **AU$200 a person**, non-refundable, and lets a group shrink by **up to 20%** without losing it ([Qantas Group Travel](https://www.qantasgrouptravel.com/media/ly0hzw0u/posau-clue-card-international.pdf)); Cathay's terms for its Middle East and South Asia markets set names at 21 days out ([Cathay Pacific](https://www.cxagents.com/content/dam/cathay-agents/lk/2026/GROUP%20BOOKING%20TERMS%20AND%20CONDITIONS_AE_IN_BH_LK_21052026.pdf)); Jetstar charges A$30–60 a passenger per flight and needs names 14 days out ([Jetstar](https://www.jetstar.com/us/en/flights/group-bookings/rest-of-world-group-bookings-terms-and-conditions); [Jetstar](https://jetstar.com/my/en/flights/group-bookings/south-east-asia-terms-and-conditions)). On an AUD 1,450 festival fare, a Singapore Airlines-style hold puts **about AUD 145 a seat at risk, against AUD 1,450 paid upfront** by an individual, and that option matters most to a household that knows it wants to go home but cannot yet say who will get leave. Two things limit it. None of Australia's own airlines flies to Kathmandu, so a Qantas, Virgin Australia or Jetstar group is interline, and Qantas then requires ticketing 45 days before departure and allows no name changes ([Qantas](https://resource.qantasgrouptravel.com/pdfs/qfdsa.pdf); [Qantas Group Travel](https://www.qantasgrouptravel.com/media/ly0hzw0u/posau-clue-card-international.pdf)). And the lock is only as good as the airline's willingness to quote: Cathay's saved group offer holds "NO Inventory" ([Cathay Pacific](https://www.cxagents.com/content/dam/cathay-agents/ae/2024/Group%20terms%20and%20conditions_AE_Oct.pdf)), Malaysia Airlines may reject any application ([Malaysia Airlines](https://www.malaysiaairlines.com/my/en/group-booking-forms.html.html)), and on peak flights displacement pricing means a price fixed a year out still reflects the airline's forecast of a full plane. Corrected, the claim reads: **the real advantage of a group fare is holding a festival price cheaply and naming travellers late, not locking in a lower price.**

## SaatSamundra's edge is early commitment, worth 10–20% against late bookers

There is a real price advantage for a group-buying app, but it is modest and depends on what each family would otherwise have done. Against a family that books the same flights early on its own, a group fare offers little or nothing, and costs more if the unverified 9% peak-date premium holds. Against a family that books inside the final two months, the point at which fares turn upward and at which 17% of Australians said they book international flights in older Skyscanner research ([Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.com.au/company-news/skyscanner-reveals-the-best-time-to-book-flights-from-australia)), an early group price saves the late climb of roughly 10–20%. The audience for that pitch is real: the usual diaspora advice is to book two to three months out ([North Nepal Trek](https://northnepaltrek.com/nepal-trip-from-australia-guide)), which for a late-September departure lands in July, just as fares into Kathmandu start rising ([Flights Nepal](https://flightsnepal.com/blog/dashain-tihar-flight-booking/)).

| If the family would otherwise... | Group price edge per seat | Family of four |
|---|---|---|
| Book the same flights early, alone | Nil, or about AUD 130 worse if the 9% peak premium holds | Nil to about AUD 520 worse |
| Book inside the final two months, normal year | About AUD 150–300 (10–20% of an AUD 1,450 fare) | About AUD 600–1,200 |
| Book late in a stressed season like November 2024 | Up to about AUD 1,170 (the gap between the cheapest and dearest Kathmandu–Sydney tickets) | Up to about AUD 4,700 |
| Join a 20-seat group that earns one free seat | About 5% off the base fare on top, taxes still payable | — |

The 10-versus-20-seat question has a two-sided answer. A free seat helps only at the larger size: where carriers publish one, it comes at one per 15–20 paid seats (LOT's sports groups get the 16th ticket free, Air Canada gives one per 20 paid, Cathay's UAE incentive groups every 16th economy passenger), with taxes still payable ([LOT](https://www.lot.com/az/en/group-booking/sport-trips); [Air Canada](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/ado/drs/agents/group-bookings.html); [Cathay Pacific](https://www.cxagents.com/content/dam/cathay-agents/ae/2024/Group%20terms%20and%20conditions_MICE_AE_Oct.pdf)), and none of the Nepal-route carriers publishes a ratio for Australia. A bigger block can also raise the per-seat price, because 20 seats displace twice as many individual sales on hub–Kathmandu legs such as Singapore Airlines' daily flight, which is narrow-body for most of the year ([Himalayan Times](https://thehimalayantimes.com/business/singapore-airlines-to-deploy-wide-body-aircraft-on-kathmandu-route-in-spring)). That is why the quote request below asks for both sizes separately.

Four conditions decide whether the edge appears. The first is ten or more travellers on one flight and date, the economy minimum at every carrier that publishes terms ([Malaysia Airlines](https://www.malaysiaairlines.com/my/en/group-booking-forms.html.html); [Singapore Airlines](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/gb/plan-travel/local-promotions/groups/IATA-Agents-TnC); [Air India](https://www.airindia.com/language-masters/en/book/group-ticket-booking.html)), which is hard with eight origin cities and a split school calendar. Victoria, Queensland and the Northern Territory break from 18 September to 3–4 October 2027, covering only Ghatasthapana on Thursday 30 September, while New South Wales, South Australia, Western Australia, Tasmania and the ACT break from 25 September to 10–11 October, which makes Dashain Tika, expected on Sunday 10 October, the last day of their holiday ([Victorian Department of Education](https://www.education.vic.gov.au/Documents/about/department/term-dates.pdf); [School Holidays Australia](https://schoolholidaysaustralia.com.au/school-holidays-and-term-dates-australia-2027/); [Kiddo](https://kiddoapp.com.au/school-holiday-dates-australia/); [days.to](https://days.to/when-is/dashain/2027)). New South Wales, home to 53% of the Nepal-born population at the 2021 Census ([ABS](https://www.abs.gov.au/articles/cultural-diversity-australia)), makes Sydney the natural first route. Tihar, 27–31 October with Laxmi Puja on the 29th and Bhai Tika on the 31st, and Chhath on 4 November fall in Term 4 everywhere, which leaves that market mostly to adults, students and retirees ([Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/nepali/tihar/tihar-calendar.html?year=2027); [Officeholidays](https://www.officeholidays.com/holidays/nepal/chhath-puja)). December gives families five to six weeks, from 11 December in Queensland and South Australia, 18 December in Victoria and 21 December in New South Wales ([Queensland Department of Education](https://education.qld.gov.au/about/Pages/futureDates.aspx); [NSW Department of Education](https://education.nsw.gov.au/schooling/calendars/future-and-past-nsw-term-and-vacation-dates)), which makes a full flight easier to assemble. The second condition is timing: ask for Dashain and Tihar quotes now, while low fare classes on the hub–Kathmandu legs are still open, and for December as schedules open between late December 2026 and late January 2027. The third is terms at least as good as the best published ones: a deposit of 10% or less, names due no earlier than three to six weeks out, and 10–20% free attrition. The fourth is access. Singapore Airlines accepts group requests only from IATA-accredited agencies ([Singapore Airlines](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/gb/plan-travel/local-promotions/groups/IATA-Agents-TnC)), Air India requires agents to put any ten or more passengers on one flight through its group desk ([Air India](https://www.airindia.com/content/dam/air-india/pdfs/air-india-gds-adm-policy-revised180923.pdf)), and Malaysia Airlines reserves the right to cancel bookings it judges abusive ([Malaysia Airlines](https://www.malaysiaairlines.com/my/en/group-booking-forms.html.html)), so the app needs an accredited agency partner and cannot quietly split a pooled group into separate bookings. Consolidator net fares are no shortcut, since they are negotiated "for blocks of seats which carriers consider hard to fill" ([AltexSoft](https://www.altexsoft.com/blog/airline-consolidator/)), which festival flights are not.

The risks fall mostly on whoever signs the contract. If SaatSamundra holds it, it carries attrition: a family that drops out after the deposit deadline costs 10% of its fare at Singapore Airlines, and once more than 20% of a Qantas group withdraws, the whole deposit is gone ([Singapore Airlines](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/gb/plan-travel/local-promotions/groups/IATA-Agents-TnC); [Qantas Group Travel](https://www.qantasgrouptravel.com/media/ly0hzw0u/posau-clue-card-international.pdf)). Fixed dates are the second exposure. Batik Air's group seats are fixed-date, fixed-flight and non-refundable ([Lion Air Group](https://lionairgroupsupport.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000637303-international-group-booking)), and the 2027 festival dates are not yet official: Vedicmarga gives Dashain Tika as Sunday 10 October and Drik Panchang as Saturday 9 October ([Vedicmarga](https://vedicmarga.com/hindu-calendar/2027/10/?view=grid); [Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/nepali/dashain/dashain-calendar.html?year=2027)), and the 2026 Tika time was announced only on 7 October 2026, two weeks before the day ([ShareSansar](https://www.sharesansar.com/newsdetail/dashain-tika-sait-fixed-for-1026-am-on-kartik-4-2026-10-07)), so a fixed-date contract needs a day of slack on either side of Tika. Disruption is the third. Cathay suspended Hong Kong–Kathmandu from 15 to 30 September 2025 after the Gen-Z protests, in Dashain season, affecting about 9,000 tickets ([Cathay Pacific](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/fr_CA/prepare-trip/travel-advisories/cathay-pacific-temporarily-suspends-kathmandu-services.html); [ekantipur](https://ekantipur.com/business/2025/10/08/en/return-of-cathay-pacific-to-nepal-tourism-sector-excited-50-18.html)); the April 2026 fuel shock pushed Kathmandu–Kuala Lumpur one-way fares from Rs40,000 to Rs170,000 ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2026/04/05/iran-war-and-soaring-fuel-prices-hit-travel-and-tourism-in-nepal-hard)); and with jet fuel near US$148 a barrel in November 2026, against about US$85 before the war, airlines' cheaper fuel hedges are expiring ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/commodities/airlines-face-higher-fuel-bills-as-pre-war-hedges-expire-and-jet-fuel-holds-near-150/)). A group price agreed now therefore needs a clear answer on whether surcharges are fixed at quote or recalculated at ticketing.

Against those risks, the opening is real and uncontested. Home Affairs counted **197,800 Nepal-born residents** in June 2024 and the ABS estimated about **213,600** a year later, although the ABS page also carries a conflicting 163,000 figure ([Home Affairs](https://www.homeaffairs.gov.au/research-and-statistics/statistics/country-profiles/profiles/nepal); [ABS](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/population/australias-population-country-birth/latest-release)); either way the community has grown well past the roughly 123,000 counted at the 2021 Census, and **71,177 Nepali nationals** studied in Australia in 2025 ([Department of Education](https://www.education.gov.au/international-education-data-and-research/international-students-studying-australia-between-2005-and-2025)). The Nepali-Australian agencies found online advertise only generic "starting from" fares, such as AUD 450–700 across ten cities ([Hifi Travels](https://hifitravels.com.au/flights-to-kathmandu/)); no group fare, block-seat or demand-pooling product for this market surfaced anywhere, though the Facebook and WhatsApp groups where informal pooling would happen could not be searched. The closest precedent, GoZayaan's Hometown app for Bangladeshi migrant workers, is built on the premise that "booking months ahead for occasions like Eid allows them to save significantly" ([Future Startup](https://futurestartup.com/2025/05/16/wafiul-haque-cbo-gozayaan-on-hometown/)).

## No group quote or live 2027 fare was obtained, so the test is still open

Neither half of the requested comparison could be completed in this research. **No airline group quote was obtained.** Airlines publish no group fares for this route and send quotes only to the person or travel agent who asks: Singapore Airlines deals only with IATA-accredited agencies, Malaysia Airlines only through its group portal, and Cathay through its agent tools ([Singapore Airlines](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/gb/plan-travel/local-promotions/groups/IATA-Agents-TnC); [Malaysia Airlines](https://www.malaysiaairlines.com/my/en/group-booking-forms.html.html); [Cathay Pacific](https://www.cxagents.com/content/dam/cathay-agents/lk/2026/GROUP%20BOOKING%20TERMS%20AND%20CONDITIONS_AE_IN_BH_LK_21052026.pdf)). **No live Google Flights fare for the 2027 dates was obtained either**, because Google Flights, the other fare sites and the airlines' booking engines could not be reached from the environment this research ran in. Every fare in this report comes from route pages, airline "from" pages and deal posts captured on 20 November 2026; none is a live quote for a 2027 date, and every 2027 figure is an estimate. The nearest thing to a Google Flights reading is its undated Sydney–Kathmandu route page, which shows December typically at A$1,600–2,600 and a cheapest one-way of A$640 on Cathay ([Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-sydney-to-kathmandu.html?gl=AU&hl=en)). What follows is everything needed to finish the test. Send the requests and run the searches on the same day, because quotes and fares both move.

### A quote request for three airlines, ready to send

Send the request to Singapore Airlines, Malaysia Airlines and Batik Air. Finally, switch on Google Flights price tracking for every search: the path of these fares between now and September 2027 is the direct test of claim two, and a 2027 fare that already sits well above its estimate means the cheap fare classes on those flights are gone.

## Conclusion

Asking whether a group fare is cheaper turns out to be the wrong test for this market. Airlines price groups so that they rarely are, and the cheapest seats go to individuals in sales that seldom touch festival dates; the money lies in when families commit and how much they must hand over to do it. That makes SaatSamundra's real competitor the family's habit of booking two or three months out, not Google Flights, and it means the app can capture much of the measurable saving without signing a group contract at all, simply by getting families to book early on their own, while keeping group contracts for flights where a quote beats the same-day individual fare or where late naming is the only way a household will sign up. December 2027 makes a better pilot than Dashain: both legs are peak, the summer holidays are long enough to fill a flight, its booking window opens within weeks, and no festival date is still waiting to be fixed.

The evidence that would settle claim two does not exist yet, and SaatSamundra is better placed than anyone to produce it. One day of quotes against Google Flights tests claim one; claim two needs the same 2027 itineraries tracked from now until departure, and that price history is what turns "book early with us" from a slogan into a number the app can show next year's families.
